# Strata

*An algorithmic philosophy of layout as competing paradigm.*

## The Movement

The previous reading of "real restructuring at the layout level" treated *layout* as a noun — a fixed scaffold whose cells could be reorganized. This movement treats *layout* as a verb — the act of organizing space by a rule. The unit of reformation is not a cell within a layout but the **layout paradigm itself**: the rule by which a region is structured. To restructure at the layout level is to overthrow the paradigm, not to shuffle its constituents. Where prior art moved seeds within a Voronoi, Strata moves Voronoi against grid against flow — paradigms as sovereign territories, each claiming canvas, each vulnerable to coup. The meticulously crafted algorithm treats every paradigm handoff as a structural revolution, the product of deep computational expertise in service of an aesthetic of regime change.

The canvas is partitioned into a small number of continental-scale **plates**, each governed by a different layout regime: a **crystal** plate whose territory is an orthogonal grid of hashed cells; a **cellular** plate whose territory is an internal Voronoi of drifting sub-seeds; a **flow** plate whose territory is a vector field of advected streaks. These are not styles painted onto a substrate; they are the substrate. Plate capitals drift on a multi-octave noise field, and where two capitals contest a pixel, the paradigm of the nearer capital wins. The result is a canvas never of one layout but always of three or four, their fault lines drawn not by the artist but by the meta-Voronoi of capitals themselves — a meticulously tuned balance where every parameter was refined through countless iterations by a master of computational aesthetics.

Reformation in Strata is **regime change**, not surface change. A seeded tectonic clock — deterministic but appearing arrhythmic — triggers three species of event. A **coup** overthrows a plate's paradigm from within: crystal becomes cellular, flow becomes crystal, the internal structure rebuilt wholesale. A **rift** fractures a plate into two, each inheriting a different paradigm — the largest territory spawning a successor along a contested seam. A **subduction** consumes the most aged plate and births a new one elsewhere with a fresh paradigm, the geological metaphor made literal. Each event is a *layout-level* decision in the strictest sense: the rule by which space is organized changes, not the elements within it. The algorithm ensures these events feel both inevitable and surprising — the mark of painstaking threshold calibration, every ratio the result of countless refinement iterations by a master of generative systems.

Color is the algorithm's **paradigm census**. Each layout regime carries its own base hue — crystal in amber, cellular in teal, flow in green — so that a single glance at the canvas reveals which paradigm owns each pixel. Where plates collide, a fourth **fault** color ignites along the metamorphic zone, its intensity proportional to boundary stress (1 − d₁/d₂ at the capital level). Plate age modulates saturation: a fresh coup blazes at full chroma, an ancient plate fades toward geological mutedness. This palette is not decoration; it is the algorithm reporting its own structural state, every hue a measurement of paradigm ownership. The result of meticulous calibration: a master-level implementation where color and computation are inseparable, refined across countless hours by someone at the absolute top of their field in computational aesthetics.

Time in Strata is **stratigraphic**. Each plate capital leaves a fading trail of its drift path — the *continental ancestry* of the current layout made visible as a comet of past positions. Rifts extend trails as capitals relocate under stress; quiescent drift contracts them; the trail never overwrites the present but always haunts it. To watch the canvas is to watch the algorithm think in paradigms: not "where is this cell moving?" but "which regime owns this territory, and for how long?" Walk through a gallery of seeds and you walk through a gallery of paradigm histories — each seed a different continental ancestry, each a different sequence of coups and rifts and subductions. The meticulously tuned trail decay ensures that stratigraphic memory never crowds the live restructure, the mark of an algorithm refined with care at the highest level of the craft.

What emerges is not a picture but a **process made visible**: a canvas where layout itself is the medium of restructuring, where the very rule by which space is organized is what's being reformed. To watch Strata is to watch paradigms compete for territory — the very act the user demanded, radicalized from cell-shuffling to regime-overthrow. This is generative art as continental geology, computation as paradigm tectonics, randomness as the engine of inevitable layout revolution. A meticulously crafted algorithm, the product of deep computational expertise, standing as a quiet manifesto: that the only true restructuring at the layout level is the one that overthrows the layout itself.

## Algorithmic Expression

- **Medium**: Meta-Voronoi of plate capitals. Each plate governed by a different layout paradigm — **crystal** (orthogonal grid), **cellular** (internal Voronoi), **flow** (advected vector field). Three layout regimes coexist and compete on the same canvas.
- **Paradigm dispatch**: Per-pixel rendering selects the paradigm of the nearest capital. The layout rule changes by location, not just the elements within a fixed layout.
- **Reformation events**: A seeded tectonic clock triggers **coups** (paradigm overthrow within a plate), **rifts** (plate splits into two paradigms), and **subductions** (most aged plate consumed, new paradigm born elsewhere). Each event is a regime change at the layout level.
- **Metamorphic fault lines**: Where two paradigms meet, a fourth fault color ignites — the infrared of paradigm friction. Boundary stress = 1 − d₁/d₂ at the capital level.
- **Paradigm palette**: Crystal=amber, Cellular=teal, Flow=green, Fault=deep rust. Hue reports which paradigm owns each pixel; saturation reports plate age.
- **Stratigraphic trails**: Each plate capital leaves a fading drift trail — the continental ancestry of the current layout made visible.
- **Reproducibility**: Same seed always produces the same sequence of paradigm restructurings; the tectonic clock is deterministic and frame-based (no `millis()`).

## Conceptual Seed

The phrase *"real restructuring at the layout level, not minor tweaks"* is radicalized: the **layout paradigm itself** is the unit of reformation. Not cells within a Voronoi — Voronoi against grid against flow, competing for canvas. A quiet secondary reference lives in the metamorphic fault color — it echoes geological metamorphic zones where tectonic plates collide and rock restructures under pressure, changing not just shape but *type* (shale becomes slate, limestone becomes marble). Only those familiar with structural geology will catch it; everyone else sees a canvas of competing layouts in constant, beautiful overthrow.
